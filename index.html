Questions, answered

A few useful details about starting a project with Atelier Veda.

What kinds of projects do you take on?

We work on homes, apartments, workplaces and hospitality spaces. The scope is shaped around the site, project needs and agreed brief.

Where do you work?

Share your site location and project scope to discuss whether the studio is a good fit.

When should I get in touch?

As early as possible, ideally before the layout, budget and consultant team are fixed. Early input can help align the brief and the site constraints.

What happens in the first meeting?

A first conversation can cover your goals, site, timing and budget range. A written proposal can then set out the agreed scope and next steps.

Do you manage construction?

Site visits and coordination can be included when they are part of the agreed project scope. Construction services and responsibilities should be set out in the signed proposal.

How do I get a fee estimate?

A useful first brief includes the site location, approximate size, intended scope and target timeline. The current contact form is a demo and does not transmit messages.
